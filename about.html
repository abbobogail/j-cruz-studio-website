About

Meet Jackie.

My story.

I was born and raised right here in Williamsburg, back when it wasn't very pretty. Growing up here teaches you that you can survive just about anything, and twenty years later, I'm back home.

That resilience carried me through the hardest chapter of my life. I went through breast cancer twice, and it changed everything. I lost a part of myself, but it made me dig deep and find another part of myself.

Afterward, I rented this space for an art event and had a quick vision that it could be a salon, a studio, a place for art. I never thought it would actually happen, and now I've celebrated two years here.

This studio has become a place of healing for me. The more I surround myself with clean products and these ideas, the more I live them, so when you come see me, you'll know I'm walking my walk, not just talking it. And it isn't just for me. Everyone who comes through here is looking for beauty wellness in some shape or form.

This is just step one, stage one, season one of a bigger vision for this studio and my art.

Part of my journey is to help other survivors. Once you go through it, something else blossoms, and you share that with the world.

The painter

Colors, colors, and more colors.

If it's not on the hair, it's on the canvas. Painting releases something for me; it's part of what has healed my spirit and my soul. Whether I'm mixing color for a client or bringing a canvas to life, it's the discovery I love. I always say the artist finds the canvas, but the canvas ends up finding the artist.
