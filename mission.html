The Mission

Beauty is wellness.

Beauty wellness from the heart, and literally through the arts.

What beauty wellness means

Beauty wellness is the whole of you: your spirit, your exterior, your interior, together. It means going nontoxic, as clean as possible, with color that complements you and products that do right by you for the long term.

i.

Clean

All products used are non-toxic, vegan, and ammonia-free. Every ingredient is read and chosen for how it treats you today and for years to come.

ii.

Release

The moment you walk in, you can release and let go. For the hour or so you're here, the outside world goes quiet.

iii.

Real

You leave feeling released and recharged, with a beauty that doesn't feel fake, just color and care that bring out who you already are.

Through the arts

Color, on hair, skin, and canvas.

Hair color, permanent makeup, restorative areola tattooing, and painting all come from the same place: a studio where you become well again, in the most organic way possible.
